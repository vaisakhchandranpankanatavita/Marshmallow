The short version

A Railway Arch
And A Bad Idea

In 2019 we took a shed off Sarjapur Road in Bengaluru, bought a second-hand four-colour carousel from a retiring printer in Tiruppur, and spent eleven months ruining cotton.

The first drop was forty shirts. We sold eleven, gave nine to friends, and wore the rest ourselves. The second drop sold out in a weekend, which we assumed was a fluke. It was not, and we have been printing ever since.

Everything still comes off that same carousel. When you order, someone here pulls a blank off the rack, lines up the screen, and pulls the squeegee — once for every colour in the design.

The honest version

What We Do
And Don't Do

We would rather tell you exactly where we are than call ourselves sustainable and hope nobody checks.

✅

What we do

Water-based inks, organic cotton from Tiruppur, plastic-free mailers, and runs of 200 so we are not burning stock nobody bought.

🚧

What we're fixing

Our blanks travel 300km from Tiruppur by road, which we are fine with — but our mailers still come from a supplier in China. We are testing an Indian alternative and the seal is not good enough yet.

❌

What we won't claim

We are not carbon neutral. We don't buy offsets to say we are. Printing and shipping clothes has a footprint and pretending otherwise helps nobody.
